# UniRAG dataset

Synthetic, fictional data for **Deccan Valley University of Technology (DVUT), Pune**. Generated by `python scripts/generate_data.py` (fixed seed 42). Ledger and grant statuses are computed as of **23 Nov 2026**.

## Contents
| Source | Size |
|---|---|
| students | 200 (CS 72, IT 64, ENTC 64; Years 1–4; first-years have no CGPA yet) |
| courses | 75 (core, lab, department and open electives, 2026-ODD) |
| enrollments | 1167 with attendance % |
| fee_ledger | 874 rows (21 overdue) |
| grants | 15 (open / upcoming / closed) · grant_applications 34 |
| policy documents | 7 Markdown files in `data/docs/` |

**By design:**
- Eligibility rules for grants exist **only** in `02_research_grant_policy.md`.
- Amounts, deadlines and statuses exist **only** in the `grants` table.
- Both are linked by `grant_code`.

## Hybrid demo & evaluation questions
Each question has a single correct answer that needs **both** the database and the policy documents.

### H1. I am a third-year CS student with a 3.8 GPA; what research grants am I eligible for, and what is the deadline to apply?

**Expected answer:** A 3.8 GPA on the 4-point scale converts to CGPA 9.50 (Handbook §4). Matching the Research Grant Policy rules (CGPA, year, department) against grant status, amounts and deadlines in the Grants Register:
  - **GR-AI-01 AI for Social Good Research Fellowship**: ₹1,50,000, apply by **20 Dec 2026** (open)
  - **GR-ML-02 Undergraduate Machine Learning Research Assistantship**: ₹60,000, apply by **10 Jan 2027** (open)
  - **GR-CY-03 Cybersecurity Research Scholarship**: ₹75,000, apply by **31 Jan 2027** (open) — only if the student has no overdue fee dues
  - **GR-SUS-08 Sustainability & Green Computing Grant**: ₹70,000, apply by **28 Feb 2027** (open)
  - **GR-HC-10 HealthTech Innovation Grant**: ₹90,000, not open yet — applications from 05 Jan 2027, deadline 15 Mar 2027
  - **GR-SP-14 Summer Research Internship Stipend**: ₹40,000, not open yet — applications from 01 Dec 2026, deadline 31 Mar 2027
  - GR-HW-06 Hardware Hackathon Seed Fund: eligible but **closed** (all awards filled)
  - GR-ROB-12 Autonomous Robotics Research Grant: eligible but **closed** (all awards filled)

*Sources needed:* Docs: Handbook §4 (conversion), Grant Policy §3 (eligibility) · SQL: grants (status, amount, deadline)

### H2. Can student S2026CS003 get the hall ticket for all end-semester exams this term?

**Expected answer:** Not automatically. Krishna Kale has paid the exam fee in full and meets the 75% attendance rule in every course except **CS102 Engineering Physics** (73.5%). Because that is between 65% and 75%, attendance can be condoned with a medical certificate and a condonation fee of ₹500 (Examination & Attendance Rules §3); otherwise the student is detained from that course's exam only.

*Sources needed:* Docs: Exam & Attendance Rules §2–§5 · SQL: enrollments.attendance_pct, fee_ledger (exam)

### H3. As of 30 November 2026, how much late fine has student S2026CS012 accumulated on overdue fees?

*Sources needed:* Docs: Fee Policy §4 · SQL: fee_ledger (status, due_date, balances)

### H4. Student S2023ENTC001 wants to add one more 3-credit elective this term. Is that allowed?

**Expected answer:** Not this term. On credits alone it would be allowed: Varun Sharma (CGPA 8.82) is registered for **25 credits** in 2026-ODD, and with CGPA ≥ 8.5 the limit is 28 credits with HoD approval, so 25 + 3 = 28 fits (Course Registration & Credit Policy §3). But the add/drop period for 2026-ODD ended on **10 Aug 2026** (§4), so no course can be added now; the student can plan it for the next term.

*Sources needed:* Docs: Course Registration Policy §3–§4 · SQL: students.cgpa, enrollments + courses.credits

### H5. Is student S2025CS017 eligible for a merit fee concession, and how much would it be?

**Expected answer:** Yes. Riya Shah has CGPA 8.60 (slab 8.50–8.99 → 15%) and no overdue fees (Scholarship & Fee Concession Policy §2–§3). The concession is 15% of the annual tuition of ₹1,45,000 in the 2026-27 fee structure for CS Year 2 = **₹21,750**, applied to next year's tuition.

*Sources needed:* Docs: Scholarship Policy §2–§3 · SQL: students.cgpa, fee_ledger (overdue check), fee_structure

### H6. Can student S2025CS003 renew their hostel room for next year?

**Expected answer:** Not at present. Krishna Iyer meets the academic condition (CGPA 7.45 ≥ 6.0) but has overdue fees: tuition_inst1 (₹87,000 due since 14 Aug 2026). Hostel Rules §3 require no overdue dues for renewal, so the room can be renewed once these are cleared before the renewal window closes.

*Sources needed:* Docs: Hostel Rules §3 · SQL: students (cgpa, is_hosteller), fee_ledger (overdue)
