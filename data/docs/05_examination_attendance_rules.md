# Examination & Attendance Rules 2026-27

**Deccan Valley University of Technology (DVUT), Pune** · Examination Cell · Effective from 1 July 2026

## 1. Scope
These rules govern attendance requirements, condonation, detention and the issue of hall tickets for end-semester examinations. Each student's attendance percentage for every registered course is recorded in their enrollment record and updated weekly.

## 2. Minimum attendance
A student must have at least **75% attendance in each registered course**, counted separately for every course, to be allowed to take that course's end-semester examination. Attendance is not averaged across courses.

## 3. Condonation of attendance
1. If attendance in a course is **at least 65% but below 75%**, the shortfall may be condoned on medical or other documented grounds.
2. The student submits a condonation application with supporting documents (e.g. a medical certificate) to the Head of Department, along with a **condonation fee of ₹500 per course**.
3. Condonation must be requested before the last teaching day of the term.
4. A student may receive condonation in at most two courses per term.

## 4. Detention
If attendance in a course is **below 65%**, or a condonation request is rejected, the student is **detained** in that course. They cannot take its end-semester examination and must re-register for the course when it is next offered. Detention in one course does not affect the student's other courses.

## 5. Hall tickets
A hall ticket for the end-semester examinations is issued only when:
1. the **examination fee for the term is fully paid** in the fee ledger (a late examination fee of ₹500 applies after the due date, see Fee Payment & Refund Policy §4); and
2. the student meets the attendance requirement (§2), or has an approved condonation (§3), in each course listed on the hall ticket.

Courses in which a student is detained are left off the hall ticket.

## 6. Conduct during examinations
1. Students must reach the examination hall 20 minutes before the start and carry their hall ticket and University ID card.
2. Electronic devices are not allowed in the examination hall.
3. Malpractice is reported to the Academic Integrity Committee (Student Handbook §7).

## 7. Results and revaluation
Results are declared within 30 days of the last examination. A student may apply for revaluation of any course within 7 days of the results through the Examination Cell portal.
