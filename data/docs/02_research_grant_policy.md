# Research Grant Policy 2026-27

**Deccan Valley University of Technology (DVUT), Pune** · Office of Research & Innovation · Effective from 1 October 2026

## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy defines who may apply for each undergraduate research grant offered at DVUT, the documents required, and how applications are selected. It applies to all undergraduate students of the Departments of Computer Science & Engineering (CS), Information Technology (IT) and Electronics & Telecommunication (ENTC).

The award amount, number of awards, application window, deadline and current status (open, upcoming or closed) of every grant are maintained in the University Grants Register (live system) and are not repeated in this policy. Where this policy and the Register differ on a date or amount, the Register prevails.

## 2. General eligibility rules (apply to every grant)
1. Eligibility is assessed on the applicant's CGPA on the 10-point scale as recorded in the latest declared result. Students who state a 4-point GPA are assessed using the conversion in the Student Handbook, Section 4 (CGPA = 4-point GPA ÷ 0.4).
2. First-year students do not yet have a CGPA and are not eligible for any grant in this policy.
3. A student may hold at most **two** research grants at the same time.
4. A student must apply before the deadline shown in the Grants Register; late applications are not considered.
5. Applications are accepted only while the grant's status in the Register is **open**. Grants marked **closed** have stopped accepting applications (deadline passed or all awards filled); grants marked **upcoming** will accept applications from their opening date.
6. Research grants may be held together with a merit fee concession (see Scholarship & Fee Concession Policy).

## 4. Selection process
1. The Office of Research & Innovation screens applications for eligibility within 10 working days of the deadline.
2. Eligible applications are reviewed by a panel of three faculty members, scoring originality (40%), feasibility (30%), impact (20%) and academic record (10%).
3. Results are published in the Grants Register. Awarded students must sign the grant agreement within 7 days.

## 5. Disbursement and reporting
- 50% of the award is released on signing the agreement and 50% after an approved mid-term report.
- Grant holders submit a final report and a poster at the Annual Research Day.
- A student whose CGPA falls below the grant's minimum during the grant period receives no further instalments.
