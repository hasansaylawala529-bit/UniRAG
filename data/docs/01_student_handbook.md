# Student Handbook 2026-27

**Deccan Valley University of Technology (DVUT), Pune** · Office of the Dean of Students · Effective from 1 July 2026

## 1. About the University
DVUT is an autonomous engineering university offering four-year B.Tech programmes through three departments: Computer Science & Engineering (CS), Information Technology (IT) and Electronics & Telecommunication (ENTC). Each academic year has two terms: the **Odd term** (Semesters 1, 3, 5, 7) and the **Even term** (Semesters 2, 4, 6, 8).

## 3. Grading system
1. Each course is graded on a 10-point scale: O (10), A+ (9), A (8), B+ (7), B (6), C (5), P (4), F (0).
2. The Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) is the credit-weighted average of grade points in a semester. The Cumulative Grade Point Average (**CGPA**) is the credit-weighted average across all completed semesters.
3. A student's first CGPA is declared after the Semester 1 results. First-year students in the Odd term therefore have no CGPA on record.
4. A course graded F must be repeated in the next term in which it is offered.

## 4. GPA conversion for external and scholarship purposes
Some scholarships, grants and external applications ask for a GPA on a 4-point scale. DVUT uses a fixed linear conversion:

- **4-point GPA = CGPA × 0.4**
- **CGPA = 4-point GPA ÷ 0.4**

For example, a CGPA of 9.5 corresponds to a 4-point GPA of 3.8, and a 4-point GPA of 3.4 corresponds to a CGPA of 8.5. All internal eligibility decisions (grants, concessions, hostel, credit overload) are made on the 10-point CGPA.

## 5. Academic standing
- **Good standing:** CGPA of 5.0 or higher.
- **Academic probation:** CGPA below 5.0 for two consecutive terms. A student on probation may register for at most 18 credits and must meet a faculty advisor every month.
- **Dean's List:** SGPA of 9.0 or higher with no F grade in that semester.

## 7. Code of conduct
1. Students must carry their University ID card on campus at all times.
2. Ragging in any form is a criminal offence and leads to expulsion.
3. Academic dishonesty (plagiarism, impersonation, use of unauthorised material in examinations) is handled by the Academic Integrity Committee. A first offence normally results in an F grade in the course concerned.

## 8. Grievance redressal
A student may raise a grievance in writing with the Head of Department. If it is not resolved within 15 working days, it may be escalated to the Dean of Students, whose decision is final for academic matters.
