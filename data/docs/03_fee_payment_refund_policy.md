# Fee Payment & Refund Policy 2026-27

**Deccan Valley University of Technology (DVUT), Pune** · Accounts Office · Effective from 1 July 2026

## 1. Scope
This policy covers how University fees are billed, paid and refunded. Fee **amounts** for each department and year of study are published in the Fee Structure 2026-27 (live system). Each student's dues, payments and due dates are recorded in their individual **fee ledger**.

## 2. Fee components
| Component | Billed | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tuition fee | Annual, in two instalments | Instalment 1 = 60% of annual tuition; Instalment 2 = 40% |
| Examination fee | Per term | Must be paid before the hall ticket is issued |
| Laboratory fee | Annual | Billed with Instalment 1 |
| Hostel fee | Annual | Only for students allotted a hostel room |

## 3. Due dates and payment
1. The due date for each component is shown in the student's fee ledger. For 2026-27, hostel fees fall due before the Odd term begins, Tuition Instalment 1 and the laboratory fee in August, the Odd-term examination fee in October, and Tuition Instalment 2 in January.
2. Payments are made online through the student portal. A receipt is generated immediately, and the ledger is updated within one working day.
3. Part-payments are accepted, but a component counts as cleared only when its full amount has been paid.
4. A ledger entry is marked **paid** when fully cleared, **pending** when not yet due, **partial** when part-paid before the due date, and **overdue** when any balance remains after the due date.

## 4. Late payment fines
1. A late fine of **₹100 per day** is charged on each overdue tuition instalment, laboratory fee and hostel fee, counted from the day after the due date.
2. The late fine is **capped at ₹5,000 per component**.
3. A late examination fee is charged a **flat ₹500**, not the daily fine.
4. Late fines are charged on top of the outstanding balance and must be paid before the component is considered cleared.
5. The Accounts Office may waive late fines only for documented medical emergencies or bereavement, on application within 30 days.

## 5. Consequences of overdue dues
A student with any overdue entry in the fee ledger:
- is not issued a **no-dues certificate**;
- is not eligible for a merit fee concession (Scholarship & Fee Concession Policy) or hostel renewal (Hostel Rules);
- is not eligible for research grants that require a no-dues certificate (Research Grant Policy);
- will have their results withheld if dues remain unpaid on the result declaration date.

## 6. Refunds on withdrawal
Refunds are calculated on the **tuition fee actually paid**, based on the date of the written withdrawal request relative to the start of classes for the term:

| Withdrawal request received | Tuition refund |
|---|---|
| Before classes begin | 90% of tuition paid, less a processing fee of ₹1,000 |
| Within 14 days after classes begin | 75% of tuition paid |
| 15–30 days after classes begin | 50% of tuition paid |
| More than 30 days after classes begin | No refund |

- Examination and laboratory fees are **non-refundable** once the term has started.
- Hostel fee refunds follow the Hostel Rules.
- Refunds are paid to the original payment account within 30 working days.
