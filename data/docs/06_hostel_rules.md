# Hostel Rules & Allotment Policy 2026-27

**Deccan Valley University of Technology (DVUT), Pune** · Hostel Office · Effective from 1 July 2026

## 1. Facilities
DVUT runs two hostels on campus: Sahyadri House (men) and Krishna House (women), with 420 beds in total in shared twin rooms. The hostel fee covers accommodation, mess (three meals a day), Wi-Fi, laundry and 24×7 security. The annual hostel fee is published in the Fee Structure.

## 2. First-year allotment
1. First-year students are allotted rooms based on distance from home: students whose permanent address is more than 50 km from campus get priority.
2. Allotment is confirmed only after the hostel fee is paid by its due date in the fee ledger.

## 3. Renewal for the next academic year
A current resident may renew their room for the next academic year only if **all** of the following hold:
1. CGPA of **6.0 or higher** (10-point scale) at the time of renewal;
2. **no overdue fee dues** of any kind in the fee ledger (tuition, examination, laboratory or hostel);
3. no more than one recorded disciplinary warning in the current year.

The renewal window runs from **1 April to 30 April 2027**. Residents who do not renew in this window lose their room, and it is offered to students on the waiting list.

## 4. Vacating and hostel fee refunds
- A resident who vacates **on or before 30 September** of the academic year receives a refund of 50% of the hostel fee paid.
- No hostel fee refund is given for vacating after 30 September.
- The security deposit is refunded in full when the room is handed over undamaged.

## 5. Rules of residence
1. Entry gates close at 22:30. Late entry needs prior permission from the warden.
2. Visitors are allowed in the common lounge only, between 16:00 and 20:00.
3. Cooking appliances, alcohol and smoking are prohibited.
4. Damage to hostel property is charged to the resident's fee ledger.
