# Course Registration & Credit Policy 2026-27

**Deccan Valley University of Technology (DVUT), Pune** · Academic Office · Effective from 1 July 2026

## 1. Scope
This policy covers how students register for courses each term, credit limits, electives, and changes after registration. Each student's registered courses, and each course's credits and capacity, are recorded in the University's course and enrollment records.

## 2. Course types
| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Core | Compulsory courses for the department and semester |
| Lab | Compulsory laboratory courses (2 credits) |
| Department elective | Chosen from the department's list (Years 3 and 4) |
| Open elective | Offered by any department, open to all Year 3 and Year 4 students |

Core and lab courses are registered automatically. Electives are chosen by the student during registration.

## 3. Credit limits per term
1. A student must register for at least **16 credits** each term.
2. The normal maximum is **24 credits** per term.
3. A student with a CGPA of **8.5 or higher** may register for up to **28 credits** (credit overload) with the written approval of the Head of Department.
4. First-year students register only the fixed first-year curriculum (16 credits).
5. A student on academic probation may register for at most 18 credits (Student Handbook §5).

## 4. Add/drop period
1. Courses may be added or dropped only during the **add/drop period, which ends 14 days after classes begin**. For the 2026-ODD term it ended on **10 August 2026**.
2. After the add/drop period, no course can be added. A student may withdraw from an elective until the last teaching day; it then appears as "dropped" and does not count towards credits.
3. Dropping a course must not take the student below the 16-credit minimum.

## 5. Elective capacity and waiting lists
1. Each elective has a fixed capacity. When it is full, further requests go on a waiting list in order of CGPA.
2. Seats freed during the add/drop period go to the waiting list automatically.

## 6. Timetable clashes
Students may not register for two courses whose timetable slots overlap. Open electives are scheduled on Wednesday and Friday afternoons so that they do not clash with department timetables.
