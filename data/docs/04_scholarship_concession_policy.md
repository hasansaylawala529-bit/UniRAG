# Scholarship & Fee Concession Policy 2026-27

**Deccan Valley University of Technology (DVUT), Pune** · Scholarship Cell · Effective from 1 July 2026

## 1. Purpose
DVUT offers merit-based fee concessions to reward academic excellence. This policy defines who qualifies, how the concession is calculated, and how it is applied. Government and external scholarships are administered separately and are not covered here.

## 2. Merit concession slabs
The concession is a percentage of **annual tuition**, determined by the student's CGPA (10-point scale) at the end of the academic year:

| CGPA | Concession |
|---|---|
| 9.00 and above | 25% of annual tuition |
| 8.50 – 8.99 | 15% of annual tuition |
| 8.00 – 8.49 | 10% of annual tuition |
| Below 8.00 | Not eligible |

The concession applies only to tuition. Examination, laboratory and hostel fees are not reduced.

## 3. Eligibility conditions
A student qualifies for the concession only if **all** of the following hold:
1. The student has a declared CGPA. First-year students in their first term have none and are not eligible.
2. The student has **no overdue entries** in the fee ledger at the time the concession is assessed.
3. The student has no F grade in the current academic year.
4. The student has not been found guilty of academic dishonesty in the current academic year.

## 4. Calculation and application
1. The concession amount is the slab percentage multiplied by the annual tuition shown in the current year's Fee Structure for the student's department and year of study.
2. It is credited against the **next academic year's** tuition, split proportionally across the two instalments.
3. A concession is not paid out in cash and lapses if the student withdraws.

## 5. Combining with other support
- A merit concession can be held together with research grants (see Research Grant Policy §2).
- It cannot be combined with any other University tuition waiver; the larger of the two applies.

## 6. Process
Eligible students are identified automatically from academic and fee records after the Even-term results. The list is published by the Scholarship Cell, and students may appeal errors within 15 days at scholarships@dvut.edu.in.
